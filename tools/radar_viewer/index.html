Design a cfg

With a fixed sample count, max range and range resolution move together (max ≈ 0.9 × samples × resolution); the same goes for velocity and the chirp count. Change the counts to trade one for the other.

Antenna calibration

    
The 2 chips' 48 virtual channels each have small phase and gain errors from trace lengths and the parts themselves.
      The cfgs carry TI's numbers from their own board; measuring yours sharpens angles and cuts ghost points.
      Put a corner reflector (~10 dBsm) at boresight, 1–12 m away, in an open room, with nothing else close to it.
